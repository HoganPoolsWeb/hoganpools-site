Our Passion:

Pools lasting as long as memories you make.

Designed around your backyard, built around your family.

Hogan Pools is a family-owned custom gunite pool builder serving Ballwin, St. Louis, and surrounding Missouri communities. Led by owner Anthony George, we design and build residential swimming pools with a hands-on approach from initial planning and excavation through construction, finishing, and startup.

Every Hogan Pools project is built around the homeowner's vision, combining thoughtful design, proven construction methods, quality materials, and clear communication throughout the entire pool-building process.

  • Custom gunite pool designs tailored to each homeowner's backyard, lifestyle, and goals
  • Complete project management from pool design and excavation through construction and startup
  • Durable structural systems, equipment, and finishes selected for long-term performance

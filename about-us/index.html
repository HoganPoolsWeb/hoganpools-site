About Hogan Pools

Generations of pool building experience, brought to your backyard.

20+ years of pool construction experience
Owner Anthony George brings two decades of hands-on pool construction experience to every Hogan Pools project.

Anthony George, Founder & Owner

Our roots go back to 1976, when Anthony's grandfather began building pools. Today, Anthony's father contributes 40+ years of hands-on expertise, helping ensure each project reflects proven craftsmanship and lasting quality.

From design collaboration and site preparation to structural, mechanical, and finish systems, we manage each phase with modern methods, quality materials, and the clear communication homeowners need from start to finish.
